# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

## Overview
**Breaking Bad** is an acclaimed crime drama television series created by Vince Gilligan. It follows Walter White, a struggling high school chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing and selling methamphetamine.

### Key Details
* **Genre:** Crime drama, Thriller
* **Seasons:** 5
* **Main Characters:** 
  * Walter White (Heisenberg)
  * Jesse Pinkman

> "I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger." — Walter White
